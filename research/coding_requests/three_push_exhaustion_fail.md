# Proposal: `three_push_exhaustion_fail`

Three-push exhaustion fail — three prior higher-highs (SHORT) or lower-lows (LONG) on `t-3..t-1`, each successive advance sized vs ATR20 by `min_push_atr` (`>=`), then bar `t` fails to make a 4th extreme **and** prints a strong reverse close. SHORT: `high[t-3] < high[t-2] < high[t-1]` AND both advances `>= min_push_atr·ATR` AND `high[t] <= high[t-1]` AND `close[t] < open[t]` AND `close[t] < close[t-1]`. LONG is the inverse (three falling lows, fail new low, strong close). Clock: `4h/4h`. Side: BOTH with SHORT priority. Family id: `three_push_exhaustion_fail` only. Garwe geometry SUPERSEDES looser Munha fail-to-extend-OR.
