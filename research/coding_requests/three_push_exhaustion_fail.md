# Proposal: `three_push_exhaustion_fail`

Three-push exhaustion fail — three successive higher-high (SHORT) or lower-low (LONG) pushes, each sized vs ATR20 by `min_push_atr`, then bar `t` fails to extend the third extreme. SHORT when three sized higher highs print on `t-3..t-1` and `high[t] <= high[t-1]`; LONG when three sized lower lows print on `t-3..t-1` and `low[t] >= low[t-1]`. Clock: `4h/4h`. Side: BOTH with SHORT priority. Family id: `three_push_exhaustion_fail` only. This is a FADE / exhaustion-after-three-pushes edge — not a consecutive-close fade, not a confirmed-pivot failed HH, not a lookback-N swing wick-fail, not a single-bar key reversal.
