The selective carry does not pass

Long spot against a short perpetual, traded only when recent funding cleared a predeclared cost hurdle, lost money on BTC, on ETH and on the combined 20,000 USDT account. It lost at baseline, at moderate and at the desk’s per-leg 0.31% round trip. A study pass required all three. The result is a fail. The passive version, which just holds the hedge, made a small gain. This is reused 2022–2025 history, not evidence of future profit, and it does not authorise live trading.

Costs

The handoff’s primary views are baseline and moderate. Severe is a stress. The assignment also asked for the desk’s 0.31% round trip. That charge is applied to each leg, so the pair pays 62 bp, not 31 bp once. Charging it once would have been a lighter test. Slippage in baseline (1 bp), moderate (5 bp) and severe (10 bp) is a label. The archive has no order-book spread.

Mean R uses the frozen 1% of equity at entry as the denominator. Losses per trade are much smaller than that 1% ceiling, so the R figures are close to zero even when the account is down about 10%. A funding-rate sum is not used as R.
