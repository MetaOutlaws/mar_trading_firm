## Diagnostic design: all 18 entry configurations
Horizon 240 minutes, matching archived trades, discovery and validation separately. Model discovery is 2023–2024; rules 2022–2024. For every entry record favourable/adverse excursion; maximum hypothetical net liquidation at minute high/low; corresponding time; net endpoint; hypothetical profit giveback; and whether net +0%, +0.5%, +1%, +2% was available BEFORE a 1% initial price stop was touched. Same-minute stop touch takes precedence over favourable excursion. Peaks are hindsight upper bounds, not executable returns. Funding at extrema follows conservative minute convention. Track whether positive peaks ended in a loss and the size of that loss. A nonpositive peak means no cost-covered opportunity in that window.

Controls: sample five eligible non-signal 15-minute decision times per real entry with seed 20261004. Match token, side, calendar quarter, UTC hour and trailing-volatility quartile. Quartile edges fit once on that token's completed 15-minute 24h-volatility feature in 2022 and remain fixed. Controls must fit the same partition and be at least 240 minutes away from every archived entry of that configuration. Sample without replacement within each matched set; reuse between sets is allowed and disclosed. If fewer than five available, use all; if none, mark unmatched. No outcome-dependent matching. Original features use only completed bars. Record control timestamps and set IDs for reproducibility.

Compare each entry with the mean of its matched controls. Cluster paired differences by the entry's UTC calendar week, resampling 5000 times with a fixed seed. Report 95% intervals and a conservative Bonferroni interval at 1-0.05/18 for the primary difference: frequency of reaching net +1% before the 1% initial stop. Control reuse and matching across weeks limit interval interpretation; these intervals are exploratory, not confirmatory. Report all 18, not only favourable comparisons.

## Conditional exit comparison: explicit new budget
This does NOT reopen PR94 Stage 3. It asks a different four-hour, paired-exit question on archived entries. Eligibility uses DISCOVERY only: at least 50 entries, >=90% matched coverage, and at least 10% of entries reach hypothetical net +1% before the initial stop. This is evidence of possible profit to protect, not a profitability gate. Among eligible configurations select at most ONE per token by discovery paired excess frequency of that event over controls; ties by experiment ID. Do not require a significant entry advantage to study exits, and report if it is absent. Freeze selected IDs to disk before evaluating any new exits, without consulting validation diagnostics for selection.

For selected configurations compare exactly four exits at a 240-minute maximum hold: (1) 1% stop / 2% target; (2) 1% initial stop, activate after +1% price excursion, trail 0.8% from extreme; (3) same stop/activation with 1.5 times entry-known 15m ATR trail, distance clamped to 0.3–2% of entry fill; (4) half exit at +1%, then same 0.8% trail on remainder. These are PR94's prewritten exit distances, unchanged; only horizon is explicitly fixed here to the diagnostic four hours. No added parameter values. At most 12 entry/exit combinations, base and doubled-slippage stress, both historical partitions. Execute identical exits on matched controls as well. No optimize-after-results step.

Use completed-bar trail updates for the NEXT minute. Test the stop known at the current bar open first; gap through fills at worse open; stop wins against target/partial on same bar. Charge entry once, fees on each exit fraction, historical funding only on held quantity. The archived execution engine is checked against independent fixtures. ATR remains entry-known/frozen. This conservative bar model cannot represent within-minute tighten-and-trigger order; report that limitation.

Archived real entries are at least four hours apart and all exits <=four hours: the identical-entry comparison is already chronological and nonoverlapping for each real configuration. Assert this. Controls may overlap and do not form a portfolio. Report counts, net mean, PF, win rate, average win/loss, max consecutive losses, additive unit-notional drawdown, holding time, activation, profit giveback, reason counts, and paired excess net over controls. Do not combine overlapping configurations or interpret additive returns as account equity.

A research candidate requires >=50 real trades, net mean>0 and PF>=1.15 in BOTH periods, positive net mean at doubled slippage in both, and positive base-cost excess net over controls in both. Any pass is exploratory only; no 2026 evaluation or promotion in this study. Report weekly-block uncertainty for real net means and matched differences.
