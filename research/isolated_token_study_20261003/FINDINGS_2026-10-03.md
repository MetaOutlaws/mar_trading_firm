The package in `research/isolated_token_study_20261003/` is an offline research baseline for Bybit USDT linear perpetuals BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, and SOLUSDT. It asks whether a rule fixed before entry can show a positive net expectancy after fees, adverse slippage, and a funding proxy, and whether that result survives a later chronological window.

This write-up is exploratory only. The run status is `exploratory_complete`. The approval string in the runner manifest is `NONE: requires independent review and existing F-kit gates`. Inbox was not opened. Live trading was not turned on. The approved book is unchanged under the Option B freeze (12 approved and 56 paper). The later audit in section 2 found the v2 unblock conditions met, and only then was `study.py` executed. Read this file for the outcome. ## 6. Material caveats

Mechanism B, `sweep_reclaim`, is adjacent to a Donchian fail-reclaim. It fades a completed 15-minute bar that sweeps the prior 16 completed bars' high or low and closes back inside. Long is the fade of a sweep below the prior low. Short is the fade of a sweep above the prior high. There is no same-bar entry. The protocol calls both mechanisms research baselines. It makes no claim that they are independent of the historical catalog. Mechanism B is not a new approved sleeve, and it is not `impulse_pullback_continuation`.

The study fee is lighter than the desk 0.31% round trip on the comparison stated in the results brief. The frozen cost assumption is 5.5 bp per side on actual entry and exit notional, which is 0.11% round trip, plus assumed adverse slippage of 5 bp per side on BTC and ETH and 10 bp per side on SOL. Adding those components is 0.21% round trip on BTC and ETH and 0.31% round trip on SOL, before funding. The repository default in `research/costs.py` (`taker_fee` 0.00055 and `slippage` 0.001) is 0.31% round trip before funding, from `CostModel.round_trip_cost_pct`. These study costs are assumptions, not measured fills. Every cell in section 5 already has a negative mean under the study schedule. The results brief's conclusion is that a desk-fee recheck would not rescue this matrix. Section 9 still lists a 0.31% sensitivity as work an external analyst can document from the exports.

Reserved 2026 was not scored, because nothing froze. Even if it had been scored, the protocol does not claim that window as an organisation-wide untouched holdout. Grokbot may already have used it.

SOL funding has historical 2-hour prints in November and December 2022. The frozen protocol places 480 of those prints from 2022-11-10 08:00 UTC through 2022-12-20 08:00 UTC, on the even hours. The v2 audit counts 480 intervals of 7,200 seconds inside the observed SOL span, together with 5,087 intervals of 28,800 seconds, and a maximum gap of 8 hours. Those 2-hour prints are not an 8-hour hole. The coverage note records the same 480 count (`funding_delta_7200` 480) and zero other irregular deltas. BTC and ETH have zero 2-hour deltas in the window.

Funding in the study is a traded-open proxy for mark price, not mark price itself. There is no liquidation model and no portfolio simulation. A same-minute stop and target is resolved as a stop. Gap stops fill at the worse open and then take adverse slippage. Target gaps do not receive a favourable improvement. If a funding settlement falls inside an ambiguous exit minute, the runner charges the larger funding cost. That is a conservative bound, not tick-accurate execution.

## 8. What was not done

No merge to production. No change to the approved book, to risk limits, to worker instructions, or to the active `impulse_pullback_continuation` task. No Inbox item. No live trading. No `approved=true`.

No scoring on 15-minute, 1-hour, or 4-hour bars. No substitution of those bars for the one-minute tape. No new protocol version after returns were seen. Gates were not loosened when every configuration failed.

## 9. Suggested next steps for an external analyst

These steps are a reading list for the exports in section 7. They are not a request to open Inbox, to loosen the gates, or to change the book.

1. Read the opportunity summary CSVs and, where a question needs the path, the gzipped opportunity windows. Keep long and short separate, and keep each horizon separate. The summary in section 5 is approximate. The CSVs are the exact cells. Use them to see how often a 3% or 5% excursion appears, and how that compares with the 1% and 1.5% stops, without treating a future high or low as a tradable entry.

2. Recompute or bound fee sensitivity at the desk 0.31% round trip. The study schedule is the 0.11% fee round trip plus the slippage in section 6. Every published cell is already negative under that lighter schedule. A 0.31% sensitivity documents the desk schedule.
