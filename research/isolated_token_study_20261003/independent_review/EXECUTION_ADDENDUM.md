# Implementation addendum, frozen before alternative returns
2026-10-03. Supplements NEXT_EXPERIMENTS.md; leaves its 20-candidate budget unchanged.

A uses simple trailing mean true range (24 completed hours), an adjust=False EMA(168), and at least 720 completed-hour warm-up observations. ATR percentage is current completed-hour ATR/close; its filter threshold is the previous 720 ATR-percent observations' median. A breakout is close above the prior 24 completed highs or below the prior 24 lows. Since the prior range includes the immediately prior candle, its close is necessarily inside that range. Only entry-known ATR is used for the 1.5 ATR stop and 3 ATR target, converted to percentages of slipped entry. Signals inside an existing position are ignored. Trade paths, funding and stop/target ambiguity use the audited baseline engine. No result-dependent filter tuning.

B fits rolling OLS beta over 720 prior completed hourly log-return observations. The current residual uses this lagged beta; z-score divides by sample standard deviation of the previous 720 residual observations, each constructed using its own lagged beta. Trigger on a crossing of +2.5 or -2.5 from inside. Beta must be finite and positive, and residual standard deviation positive. Long-token means a negative residual trigger with a short BTC hedge; short-token means the opposite. Gross entry notional is 1, allocated token=1/(1+beta), BTC=beta/(1+beta). Beta and quantities are frozen until exit; no dynamic hedge turnover is hidden.

B0 holds 4h unless the package loss stop fires. B1 exits at the first later completed hourly z-score at or through zero (z>=0 for long-token; z<=0 for short-token), otherwise 4h. The gross package loss stop of -1% is checked at synchronized minute OPEN quotes, starting one minute after entry. This misses intraminute breaches and is explicitly a minute-sampled prototype, not exchange-stop parity. Stop execution takes adverse slippage on both legs. A normalization or time exit also occurs at a synchronized minute open. Funding is charged on both legs over (entry, exit], with traded minute-open price as mark proxy. Entry at a settlement excludes that settlement, consistent with the baseline convention; actual exchange timing needs separate verification. Tick-order uncertainty, asynchronous hedge fills and liquidation are not simulated.

Base/slippage/fees follow original protocol (BTC/ETH 5bp and SOL 10bp slippage/side; all 5.5bp fee/side). Report two cost levels: base and doubled slippage, with full simulation. Stress is a robustness check, not another candidate from which to cherry-pick. Both families stay flat at partition boundaries and exclude starts whose full maximum holding window crosses a partition. Research is offline, with fixed unit notional and no leveraged equity curve.

Discovery and reused validation are scored for all 20 configurations. Freeze at most one per traded token, requiring >=50 trades and positive mean/PF>=1.15 in BOTH periods at base costs and positive mean in BOTH periods at doubled slippage. Selection ranks validation base mean only. If no one qualifies, leave selection empty and do not score 2026. If candidates qualify, lock IDs before 2026 evaluation, use 99% weekly-block intervals and matched controls before proposing paper confirmation. Nothing promotes automatically.

All 2025 results are reused evidence and 2026 has partial opportunity-summary exposure. The tests can disprove these candidates; they cannot manufacture a pristine holdout after prior research has occurred.
