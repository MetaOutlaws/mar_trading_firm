# MAR additional-token comparison — 9 October 2026

Experiment: **H-RETEST-EXPANSION-01**. Status: **completed and independently verified**.

The 2025 evaluation favors the retest challenger on both completed-trade quality
and original-signal return. The overall predeclared result is **inconclusive**:
the challenger remains loss-making in 2022–2024, and only 20 challenger trades
completed in 2025. This is independent execution of the owner's frozen rules;
no Grok findings or outputs were used.

## Primary result

One-minute observed-quote exits, doubled slippage of 0.20% per side, 0.055% fees
per side, supplied funding and the frozen runtime-stop-fill cost convention.
Each arm has its own portfolio book with the same admission limits.

Every admitted primary trade completed; there are **zero boundary marks** in
these four cells. A win means positive net return after costs and funding.
Net/trade divides by completed trades. Net/original signal divides by all 157
historical or 50 evaluation signals, counting unfilled/rejected entries as zero.
These are returns on trade notional, **not compounded account returns**.

The 2025 challenger improves mean net/trade by **1.1310 percentage points** and
net/original signal by **0.6626 points**. Historical improvement is only
0.0399 points/trade, with both arms still negative. Neither historical losses
nor the small evaluation sample is removed from the conclusion.

of shared trades, so the stop-count difference is not solely the skipped stops.

## Uncertainty and prespecified sensitivities

The evaluation challenger has **20 completed trades across 18 dates, 16 entry
weeks and 13 tokens**, below the frozen 100-trade/50-date/26-week thresholds.
Its stressed 95% paired weekly bootstrap mean-return interval includes losses:
**−0.5813% to +1.7108%** for one-week blocks, seed 20261007. The corresponding
per-trade improvement interval is **+0.2377 to +1.8588 percentage points**.
Four-week stressed bounds for improvement are **+0.3521 to +1.7311 points**.
Both registered seeds are retained in the accompanying 112-row interval table.

The stressed improvement lower bounds are positive at both block lengths and
seeds. At base slippage, however, the one-week improvement interval crosses
zero for both seeds. Challenger absolute-profitability bounds cross zero in
all registered evaluation interval settings. The required joint gate fails.

do not present this ratio as a reliable percentage of profits captured.
