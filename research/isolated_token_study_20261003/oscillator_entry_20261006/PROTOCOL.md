Long RSI recovery: previous completed entry-clock RSI14 <=30, current >30. Short: previous >=70, current <70. Connors RSI uses (Wilder price RSI3 + Wilder streak RSI2 + strict percent rank of current one-bar return against preceding 100 returns)/3; long crosses from <=10 to >10, short from >=90 to <90. Regime at extreme bar need not align. Fill at next minute open after the recovery candle closes. Equality rules above are fixed. One crossing per excursion, but further excursions may create new candidates.
