# Relationship to prior work

The original trade anatomy/entry-quality studies evaluated earlier strategy
families,other stop conventions and broader timeframes. ADX,RSI and Connors gates
were tested separately;RSI on the approved combination found no incremental
benefit. Those results remain intact. This is the first planned winner/loser
diagnostic of the final approved combination using the reconciled fill-origin,
sampled-exit costs,including known2026 outcomes.

It is a descriptive conditional diagnostic,not evidence that an indicator or
median threshold is new. A historical nomination from12 features and both
directions is multiple exploratory comparison,not confirmatory testing.
The saved rule prevents choosing a lead because2025–26 happens to look good.
Full later-period feature tables are released only after the nomination is
published. Existing outcomes were seen in prior experiments.

No access to unpublished external Grok experiments is assumed. GitHub research
branch and all preserved checkpoints remain the authoritative lineage.
