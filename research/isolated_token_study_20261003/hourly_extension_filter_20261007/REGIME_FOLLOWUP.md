# Historical improvement retained; regime explanation pending

Owner clarification recorded 7 October 2026, after the completed fixed-cap test.
Label: **Historical improvement (2022–24); candidate for regime analysis**.
This finding is preserved even though the cap failed as an unconditional
replacement for the original hourly compression baseline.

| Period | Baseline mean net base / stress | Extension-cap mean net base / stress | Baseline / filtered trades |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022–24 | +0.2268% / +0.1037% | +0.3109% / +0.1834% | 97 / 60 |
| 2025 | +0.4992% / +0.3685% | +0.1999% / +0.0635% | 36 / 22 |

The historical stressed improvement is about 0.0797 percentage points per trade.
This is meaningful descriptive evidence to retain. It is not a tested rule for
switching the cap on when a market resembles a calendar period. The historical
aggregate also contains mixed results: 2023 stressed mean worsens from -0.4624%
to -0.5563%. A whole-year label of trending or choppy is not established here.
The hypothesis that 2025 chop caused the difference is untested; do not cite it
as fact or infer a profitable regime from calendar membership alone.

## Registered follow-up H-EXT-REGIME-01

Question: do observable, entry-time market conditions explain when the fixed
one-ATR cap helps and when it removes valuable breakouts?

Retain the fixed cap and its complete results. A later, separately registered
study should define trend/chop or volatility states using only completed data,
then compare baseline and cap WITHIN the same states in both 2022–24 and 2025.
Keep state definitions common across periods; record sample sizes, stop-outs,
excluded winners, uncertainty and any occupancy changes. Do not choose a state
because its completed trades won, or label all of 2022–24 as one state.

Entry-time ADX/efficiency observations may help formulate that study, but any
selection using these already reviewed outcomes remains exploratory. Confirm a
result with new-token or prospective evidence before treating it as a dependable
switch. No state definition, threshold, regime-conditioned backtest or switch
has yet been frozen, scored or deployed. Review this hypothesis after the queued
independent BTC-direction and indicator comparisons; do not lose it in the queue.

The original protocol, results and failed unconditional-improvement decision
remain intact. The approved paper baseline is unchanged.
